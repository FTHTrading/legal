Discipline coverage

Not every discipline lives in a single tab — each is threaded across all six sections. This is the map.

Real Estate

Multifamily, hospitality, industrial, storage, CMBS tranching, construction-draw escrow, tokenized REIT + fractionalized single-asset structures.

Private Credit

Direct lending, mezzanine, distressed, revenue-based finance, trade finance, invoice factoring, and tokenized credit funds.

Private Equity & VC

Buyouts, growth equity, venture capital — NVCA model docs, SAFEs, token warrants, side letters, LPA framework.

Stablecoins

GENIUS Act (2025) framework, payment vs. security classification, 1:1 reserve regime, OCC + state pathways, foreign issuer rules.

Digital Securities

ERC-3643 (T-REX), ERC-1400 partitioned tokens, ONCHAINID identity, transfer-restriction contracts, compliance modules.

Precious Metals & Commodities

Vault-backed gold/silver programs, LBMA good-delivery, warehouse receipts, oil & gas royalty tokenization.

Energy & Infrastructure

Solar SPV structures, PPA-backed offtake, SREC monetization, BESS financing, IRA/OBBBA tax credit optimization.

Sports, Media, IP

Athlete NIL vehicles, music-catalog royalty streams, film-slate financing, IP-licensing SPVs, brand-royalty tokens.

Federal & Grant Capital

SAM.gov integration, UEI/CAGE verification, ITC/PTC monetization, grant-flow routing, government-contract securitization.
