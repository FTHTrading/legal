Section 05 · Active Registry

Live RWA protocols & tokenized issuers.

Public registry of RWA protocols and tokenized-issuance vehicles currently in production. Every entry: what it does, regulatory posture, scale indicator, primary link. Not investment advice; not an endorsement.

Scale indicators are best-effort snapshots

TVL / AUM figures below are reported values as of publication. They move constantly. Always verify against the protocol's own dashboard or SEC filings before relying on any number for decision-making.

Tokenized US Treasuries & Money-Market Funds

BlackRock BUIDL Reg D 506(c) + 3(c)(7)

BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund. Tokenized fund with Securitize as transfer agent. Multi-chain (Ethereum + Aptos + Arbitrum + Avalanche + Optimism + Polygon). Ondo, Superstate, and others integrate BUIDL as reserve asset.

Scale: ~$2.7B AUM (as of Aug 2026) · Structure: BVI-domiciled entity, exempt from the 1940 Act, offered under Reg D 506(c) with 3(c)(7) exclusion · Regulator: SEC (Reg D); BVI FSC (entity)

Note: BUIDL is not a registered 1940 Act investment company. Do not cite it as precedent for a registered-fund path.

Link: securitize.io/institutional/blackrock

Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund (BENJI) SEC 1940 Act

First SEC-registered money market fund with a public blockchain (Stellar + Polygon) as the official share ledger. Franklin Templeton is the fund manager. Tokenized shares carry equivalent legal rights to traditional shares.

Scale: ~$400M+ AUM · Structure: Registered 1940-Act fund · Regulator: SEC

Link: franklintempleton.com

Ondo OUSG & USDY Reg D + Reg S

OUSG: tokenized short-duration US Treasuries for accredited/institutional. USDY: tokenized US Treasury exposure for non-US persons under Reg S. Multi-chain distribution.

Scale: ~$1B+ combined TVL · Structure: Regulated feeder to BlackRock BUIDL and comparable underlying · Regulator: SEC (Reg D); offshore Reg S

Link: ondo.finance

Superstate USTB & USCC Reg D / Accredited + QP

USTB: short-duration US Treasury fund with on-chain share registry (recently branded Invesco Short Duration US Government Securities Fund). USCC: tokenized short-duration credit strategy. Founded by Robert Leshner (Compound founder).

Scale: ~$767M combined AUM (as of Aug 2026) · Structure: Delaware Statutory Trust; Accredited + Qualified Purchaser only · Regulator: SEC (Reg D)

Note: USTB is not registered under the Investment Company Act. Per Superstate's own materials: "The Fund is not registered as an investment company." Do not cite as a registered-fund analog.

Link: superstate.com

Hashnote USYC CFTC-registered

Tokenized short-duration credit / T-bill exposure. Structured through a Cayman feeder and US-domiciled master fund. Hashnote acquired by Circle in early 2025.

Scale: Growing quickly post-Circle acquisition · Structure: Fund-of-funds with tokenized share class · Regulator: CFTC / Cayman CIMA

Link: hashnote.com

On-Chain Credit Protocols

Goldfinch In restructuring

Status (Aug 2026): governance proposal GIP-87 passed 23 June 2026. Goldfinch Prime is shutting down; the legacy protocol has entered maintenance mode; development stopped; a trust under a Chief Restructuring Officer handles legacy pool recoveries. Do not rely on historical yield figures for structuring or precedent purposes.

Historical description: uncollateralized loans to emerging-market credit borrowers via senior/junior tranche structure with delegated underwriting. ~$100M+ historical originations. Included here for reference only.

Link: goldfinch.finance

Centrifuge Multi-jurisdictional

Asset-backed credit tokenization. Real-world receivables (invoices, mortgages, trade finance) tokenized into pools. V3 launched 2024 with permissionless-market-creation focus. Aave RWA integration since 2022.

Scale: ~$500M+ historical TVL · Structure: Multi-jurisdictional SPV pools · Regulator: Varies by pool sponsor

Link: centrifuge.io

Maple Finance Reg D + Cayman

Institutional undercollateralized lending. Pool delegates underwrite borrowers (crypto market makers, prime brokers). Post-2022 defaults, pivoted to stricter underwriting and diversified strategies including Maple Direct (RWA-collateralized).

Scale: ~$400M+ TVL · Structure: Pool per delegate · Regulator: Delegate-specific

Link: maple.finance

Clearpool Cayman + Delaware

Single-borrower unsecured pools with dynamic interest rates based on utilization. Institutional borrowers KYC'd. Portal for regulated deployment; permissionless pools also exist.

Scale: ~$100M+ TVL · Structure: Per-borrower pools · Regulator: Cayman + underlying jurisdictions

Link: clearpool.finance

Aave Horizon Institutional

Aave's institutional RWA lending pool. Permissioned participation (KYC'd institutions only). Focus on tokenized T-bills and money-market fund shares as primary collateral. Aave DAO-governed but operationally institutional.

Scale: Newer product; TVL scaling · Structure: Isolated permissioned pool · Regulator: Depends on participant

Link: aave.com

TrueFi Reg D / Cayman

Unsecured lending to KYC'd institutional borrowers. Earlier entrant that pivoted after 2022 defaults. Now operating with stricter underwriting + capital-provider insurance offering (Adagio Capital).

Scale: Reduced from peak; ~$50M+ TVL · Structure: Pooled lending vaults · Regulator: Various

Link: truefi.io

Stablecoin Issuers

Circle (USDC) State trust + GENIUS-transitioning

Second-largest USD stablecoin by market cap. Backed by cash + short-dated Treasuries. Reserves at BNY Mellon + BlackRock-managed government money-market fund. Monthly attestations by Deloitte. Positioned as GENIUS Act compliant issuer.

Scale: ~$40B+ market cap · Chartering: NY DFS + state MTLs · IPO: Public company since 2025 (NYSE: CRCL)

Link: circle.com

Tether (USDT) Offshore

Largest stablecoin by market cap. Bahamas-based issuer. Quarterly attestations by BDO. GENIUS Act path uncertain — foreign-issuer compliance pathway pending Treasury equivalence determinations.

Scale: ~$120B+ market cap · Chartering: BVI + El Salvador registrations · Regulator: Multi-jurisdictional

Link: tether.to

PayPal PYUSD NY DFS Trust

PayPal's USD stablecoin, issued by Paxos Trust under NY DFS supervision. Available in PayPal + Venmo apps; multi-chain distribution (Ethereum + Solana + Arbitrum).

Scale: ~$1B+ market cap · Chartering: NY DFS (Paxos) · Regulator: NY DFS

Link: paypal.com/pyusd

Paxos (USDP, PYUSD, Global Dollar) NY DFS Trust + Singapore MAS

Regulated stablecoin infrastructure provider. Issues USDP, PYUSD (for PayPal), and Global Dollar (institutional). Multi-jurisdictional licenses: NY DFS trust, Singapore MAS, ADGM.

Scale: ~$5B+ across issued stablecoins · Chartering: NY DFS trust + MAS + FSRA (ADGM) · Regulator: NY DFS

Link: paxos.com

Ethena USDe / sUSDe Novel structure

"Synthetic dollar" backed by delta-neutral crypto + basis-trade yield. Not a fiat-backed stablecoin. sUSDe (staked variant) captures the yield. Rapidly grew to top-5 dollar-denominated crypto asset.

Scale: ~$5B+ market cap · Structure: BVI issuer + delta-neutral crypto positions · Regulator: BVI FSC; unregulated in US as of publication

Link: ethena.fi

What's not on this list

This registry is deliberately curated. Excluded categories:

  • Unregistered "RWA" tokens with no clear legal issuer. If we can't identify the legal entity, custody arrangement, and regulatory posture from public sources, it's not on the list.
  • Speculative "RWA-narrative" tokens with no underlying asset. A token that trades on the RWA story but has no backing is not RWA.
  • Purely-DeFi money markets with no real-world collateral. Aave, Compound, Morpho core markets are DeFi credit, not RWA (unless a specific pool has RWA collateral).
