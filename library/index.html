How to use the library

Every brief is standalone. Every claim is cited to the operative statute, regulation, or industry document. Nothing is opinion. Where the law is ambiguous, we say so.

These briefs are reference material for operators. They are not legal advice. They are the background reading you should have done before your first phone call with securities counsel.
