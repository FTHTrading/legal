# Not Legal Advice — UPL Notice

This repository publishes documentation for an open-source document-assembly
and diligence engine. Nothing in this repository constitutes legal advice.

## What this system does

- Assembles documents from pre-audited templates by substituting user-supplied facts
- Retrieves current text of regulatory sources on demand
- Runs parallel screens against public federal databases
- Packages provenance (template source, fact source, cited regulations, retrieval timestamps)
- Computes state notice-filing manifests from a reference dataset
- Tracks recurring compliance obligations against known statutory deadlines

## What this system does NOT do

- Render legal opinions on whether an exemption applies to specific facts
- Advise whether a specific investor qualifies as accredited
- Advise whether a Rule 506(d) disqualifying event applies to a covered person
- Draft opinions of counsel or reliance letters
- File anything with any regulator on behalf of any operator
- Provide tax, ERISA, or investment advice
- Represent that any output is legally sufficient without attorney review

## The attorney review requirement

Every document generated by this system ships with a **Review Manifest**
containing template provenance, fact sources, cited regulations, attached
diligence packets, and a structured attorney sign-off block.

The attorney signing that manifest is the person of record for the legal
opinion. The system is a scrivener and diligence engine — not counsel.

## Jurisdictional scope

Every US state prohibits the unauthorized practice of law (UPL) by
non-attorneys. Users of this system are responsible for engaging licensed
counsel in every jurisdiction relevant to their matter (formation state,
principal office state, every state where investors reside, and any state
where the operator or its personnel solicit or transact).
